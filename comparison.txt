# Performance Comparison

| Search ID | BST Comparisons | Linear Search Comparisons | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| A102 | 1 | 1 | Equal |
| A120 | 3 | 6 | BST faster |
| A45 | 4 | 8 | BST faster |
| B12 | 5 | 5 | Equal |
| B3 | 6 | 7 | BST faster |
| A7 | 3 | 3 | Equal |

## Observation

- BST is faster for A120, A45 and B3 in this experiment.
- BST and linear search use the same number of comparisons for A102, B12 and A7.
- The ordinary BST is unbalanced for the given insertion order, so its performance is not always close to O(log n).
